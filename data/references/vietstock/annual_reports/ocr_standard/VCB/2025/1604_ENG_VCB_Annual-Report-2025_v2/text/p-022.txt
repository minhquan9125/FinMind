                                                                                                                                  Overall Assessment     In 2025, the global economy continued to face significant uncertainties and challenges,
                                                                                                                                                         including the reshaping of global supply chains driven by reciprocal tariff policies of the US,

                                                                                                                                  Board of Directors’    Against this backdrop, under the decisive, proactive, and flexible leadership of its management,
                                                                                                                                                         and guided by a strategy of safe, efficient, and sustainable growth, Vietcombank successfully
                                                                                                                                                         fulfilled all assigned business targets and objectives. The bank maintained its position as a key
                                                                                                                                                         financial institution, playing a leading role in supporting economic stability and ensuring the
                                                                                                                                                         safety of the monetary and banking system.

                                                                                                                                                         During the year, Vietcombank continued to implement its action motto “Innovation –
                                                                                                                                                         Efficiency – Sustainability” alongside its management principles of “Responsibility – Discipline
                                                                                                                                                         – Connectivity – Creativity.” As at 31 December 2025, all core business indicators were
                                                                                                                                                         completed in line with targets approved by the GMS and the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV).



                                                                                                                                  Business Performance   • Total assets exceeded VND2.4 quadrillion, representing a 17% increase compared to the end
                                                                                                                                                           of 2024.
                                                                                                                                  Highlights

                                                                                                                                                           serve as the primary growth driver. Total customer deposits (Market 1) reached approximately
                                                                                                                                                           VND1.7 quadrillion, up 10.4% year-on-year, fulfilling 100% of the annual plan. Notably, current
                                                                                                                                                           account savings accounts (CASA) recorded strong growth of over 13.5%.

                                                                                                                                                         • Total outstanding credit reached approximately VND1.7 quadrillion, up 15%(1) year-on-year,
                                                                                                                                                           within the credit growth limit assigned by the SBV. The credit portfolio continued to shift
                                                                                                                                                           toward a safer and more efficient structure. Both wholesale and retail segments achieved
                                                                                                                                                           solid growth, with retail lending accounting for approximately 51% of the total loan book.
